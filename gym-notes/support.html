GymNote - Workout Tracker Support

For support, bug reports, or feedback, contact:

Ilia Kaziamov, I/E ILIA KAZIAMOV, Georgia
Email: support@kaziamov.com
Response language(s): English
Expected response time: Within 3 business days

Before contacting support

  • Include the app version shown in Settings.
  • Include your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Describe the exact steps that reproduce the issue.
  • Do not send private workout data unless it is necessary and you choose to do so.

Data recovery

GymNote - Workout Tracker does not maintain cloud copies of your data. The developer cannot restore data after the app is deleted or after "Erase all app data" is used. Export a JSON backup before deleting the app or changing devices.
