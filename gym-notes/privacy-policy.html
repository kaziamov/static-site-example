GymNote - Workout Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Summary

GymNote - Workout Tracker is a local-only workout journal. The app does not require an account and does not send personal data, workout records, or selected photos to the developer or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the name you enter, exercise catalogue entries, machine names, selected machine photos, workout dates, notes, sets, repetitions, and weight in the app's local storage on your device.

Data collection and sharing

GymNote - Workout Tracker does not operate a backend, cloud sync service, analytics service, advertising system, or tracking technology. The developer does not collect or share data from the app.

Photo selection

The app uses Apple's system photo picker. It receives only the photo that you explicitly select. The selected copy is stored locally inside the app.

Backups

You can export a JSON backup using the iOS document exporter. The destination and any later sharing are controlled by you and by the service you choose in the system share interface.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you erase it in the app or delete the app. The Settings screen provides an option to erase all app data. Deleting the app also removes its local data unless you previously exported a backup.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app later adds cloud services, analytics, accounts, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
